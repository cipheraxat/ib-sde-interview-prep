# Trap questions & red lines

Memorize these so you never walk into a wording trap.

## Instant clarifications

### TWS

**Wrong:** IB Trader Workstation experience.  
**Right:** IBM Tivoli Workload Scheduler — enterprise batch orchestration.

Say in opener: *“TWS on my resume means IBM Workload Scheduler, not IB’s Trader Workstation.”*

### Domain

**Wrong:** “I built trading / order-matching systems.”  
**Right:** “Payment and treasury **integration** backends in a regulated bank.”

### Ownership

**Wrong:** “I migrated the whole platform alone.”  
**Right:** “I contributed to the program and owned integration services / recon / replay for my areas.”

### LLM

**Wrong:** “The agent auto-remediates production.”  
**Right:** “Retrieval-grounded suggestions with **human-in-the-loop**; no unattended prod changes.”

### Kafka

**Wrong:** “Exactly-once everywhere.”  
**Right:** “At-least-once delivery with idempotent consumers; outbox to avoid lost events.”

### Proof of tokenization

**Wrong:** “The batch job was green.”  
**Right:** “Recon join to vendor snapshot requiring ACTIVE tokens for the expected population.”

---

## Soften phrases (use when unsure)

- “I don’t recall the proprietary class name; the design was …”  
- “Security/architecture owned DPaaS; I owned the integration design and recon.”  
- “On the pilot set we measured …”  

## Never invent

- Fake order books, FIX engines, market data stacks at Barclays  
- Fake team sizes or fake “I led 20 engineers”  
- Exact confidential vendor product internals
