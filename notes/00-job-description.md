# Interactive Brokers — Software Engineer

**Apply:** https://jobs.dayforcehcm.com/en-US/ibgllc/CANDIDATEPORTAL/jobs/36  
**Company:** Interactive Brokers Software Service India (IBSSI)  
**Location:** Mumbai, India (portal); Pune listed on some IBSSI postings

## Role summary

Software developers with a **Java** concentration and experience in **Unix/Linux** environments to improve customer-facing trading applications and back-end systems. Heavily oriented toward designing, building, and optimizing trading and back-office applications.

## Minimum qualifications

- MCA/BE/BTech/MTech/ME in Computer Science or related field
- 2+ years relevant experience (large systems or client application development)
- **Java** and **Oracle** / **MySQL**
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- **SQL**, relational **database** systems
- **Unix/Linux** environments
- Fluency in English

## Preferred qualifications

- Web and/or non-web **Java** applications; **Oracle** / **MySQL** **database**
- 1+ years in **Financial Services** (**Brokerage** / **capital markets** / Investment Banking)
- **Java** and **Python** with performance optimization
- Scripting: **Unix Shell**, **Linux**; **Perl**
- Scalable applications using **Kafka**
- **REST**-based **web services**
- Production troubleshooting
- **Git** and **Jenkins**
- **PL/SQL**
- **front-end** systems or corresponding **back-end** services
